Tokens: dark-mode `--accent-hover` is now `--cobalt-600` (was `--cobalt-400`, which gave white button text only 3.68:1 contrast and failed WCAG AA) and `--accent-pressed` is now `--cobalt-700`. Primary buttons hover darker instead of lighter.

New tokens (dark and light): `--accent-bar`, `--text-link`, `--text-link-hover`, `--focus-color`, `--focus-width`, `--focus-offset`, `--focus-ring`, `--border-strong-hover`, `--border-danger`, `--tint-subtle`, `--tint-default`, `--tint-strong`, `--bg-scrim`, `--ease-out`, `--duration-fast`, `--duration-base` (0ms under `prefers-reduced-motion`), and `--measure`.
